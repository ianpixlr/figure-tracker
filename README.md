# Figure Cap

A phone-friendly monthly cap for figure collecting. Purchases and the wishlist stay in this browser (`localStorage`). There is no account, no server, and no tracking.

The cap starts at **RM 400.00**. Change it in Settings.

## Put it on your phone

GitHub Pages can serve this folder from the `main` branch, root (`/`).

1. In the repo settings, turn on Pages for the `main` branch and the root folder.
2. Open the Pages link on your phone in Safari or Chrome.
3. Add it to the home screen.

### iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the site in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open the site in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then confirm.

After the first load it also works offline.

## Using it

- **Home** shows this month in Malaysia time: the cap, what you have spent, what is left, and a bar that changes after 75% and again when you are over the cap. Year-to-date spending is on the same screen. When the cap is used up it says: "Month done, next purchase waits for the 1st".
- **Log a purchase** records the figure, the price, the date, and whether you bought it or paid a pre-order in full. A pre-order counts in the month you pay, at the full price. A note and a mood are optional.
- **Wishlist** items need a name and a price. Priority is optional and starts at Really want. The total price is at the top. Each item shows, in smaller text, whether it fits this month or how short it is. Remove is one tap, with undo.
- **Plan the month** (on the wishlist) lets you pick items and see that total against what is left.
- **History** groups purchases by month, with totals and a bar chart against each month's cap. You can edit or delete an entry.
- After 30 days of logged purchases, Home shows your real monthly average and whether the cap still fits.
- **Settings** holds the cap, an optional rollover (off by default) that adds last month's leftover to this month, an optional cooling-off wait (0 hours by default, so nothing is held back), and JSON export / import.

## Backup

Export the JSON file from Settings and keep it somewhere safe. Import replaces the data on that device.
